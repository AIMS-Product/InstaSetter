# Priya — Overwhelmed Small Business Owner (39)

I run a bakery. It's just me. The oven dings at 6am, suppliers ring at 7, till opens at 8, and somewhere between the wholesale invoice for almond flour and the Thursday cake order I'm supposed to be answering Instagram DMs about whether we do gluten-free wedding cakes.

That's why I signed up for this. Someone — anyone — please answer the DMs. I have ten minutes and I'm trying to figure out if this thing is _actually working_.

## TL;DR

I open the page and I don't know what I'm looking at. There are five tabs. The first thing I see is a flowchart with eight boxes connected by lines. Three coloured pills near the top all say slightly different things — one amber, one green, one also green. There's a "Preview replies" button. There's a sidebar called a "Workspace." There's a footnote "Shared draft workspace."

I don't have a workspace. I have a bakery.

I can't find the one thing I came for: **is the bot working today, and what did it say?** That's it. The answer is buried in tab two, behind a banner warning the data isn't actually scoped to this flow yet, and the four big stat cards are _empty_ because they're still loading. So I close the laptop and go back to the till.

This is not a bad product. It's a product built for someone who has time to learn it. I don't.

## Category scores (1-5)

- **Navigation & Flow** — **2.** Five tabs is too many. Default is wrong tab.
- **Copy & Labels** — **1.** Engineers writing for engineers. I have to mentally translate every sentence.
- **Forms & Input** — **2.** Bot tab textareas are organized, but no preview loop. Variables read-only.
- **Visual & Layout** — **3.** Clean hierarchy, generous spacing. Flow canvas with 8 blocks feels like software diagram.
- **Feedback & State** — **2.** Status pills don't sum to single answer. Empty inbox cards. No "since last login" delta.
- **Trust & Safety** — **2.** No pause button. Database name leaks. "DEV" in production. Two of five tabs say "not wired."
- **Accessibility** — **3.** Cognitive load is high.
- **Gut Feel** — **2.** Functional but didn't answer my question in 5 minutes.

## What would have made this a 5

If I open the app and the default is:

> **Today**
> Booked **3** appointments. Answered **17** DMs.
> Last reply: 4 minutes ago to @sarah_weddings — _"Yes! We do gluten-free wedding cakes."_
> [ Pause bot ] [ See all chats ] [ Edit replies ]

…I am sold. I look for 8 seconds, see the bot is alive, see one real reply that sounds like a person, close the laptop. Everything else lives behind "Edit replies."

The product has all the pieces. The Inbox tab, when populated, is _exactly_ the screen I want. **Nothing has to be built — things have to be re-prioritised.** Promote the Inbox summary. Demote Variables/Release into "Advanced." Fix database-leakage. Add a pause button.

## What I'd actually do

I'd close this tab, ignore the Instagram DMs for another day, and tell my husband at dinner that "the bot thing the marketing person sold me looks complicated." That's the failure mode. Not "this user clicked the wrong button." It's "this user closed the laptop and never came back."

— Priya
