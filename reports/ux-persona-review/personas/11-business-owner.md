# Persona 11 — Priya, the Overwhelmed Small Business Owner

**Profile:** 39 years old. Owns a bakery. Runs ordering, marketing, accounting, HR. Uses this app in 10-minute windows between customers walking in. Cognitive load is maxed out the moment she opens the laptop.

**Lens:** She paid (or is considering paying) for an Instagram DM automation tool because she doesn't have time to answer DMs herself. She wants to open the app, see "is it working, is it making me money, does anything need me right now," and close the laptop. Anything that doesn't answer those three questions in the first 10 seconds is a waste of her cognitive budget.

**Pre-read mood:** Already tired. Ovens running. Front-of-house short-staffed today. The app better earn its place on her screen.

---

## Overall Gut Feel: 1 / 5

**Justification:** This doesn't look like a product for a business owner. It looks like a dev tool someone handed me by mistake — a giant flow-builder canvas, no numbers, no "here's what your bot did today," and when I click the one button that sounds like it might show me customer conversations, the app crashes with "Something went wrong." I would close the tab and never come back.

## Category Summary

- **Navigation & Flow:** Broken. No nav, no breadcrumbs, dead ends everywhere, two of three user-facing pages are either wrong (dashboard is empty) or crashed (conversations).
- **Copy & Labels:** Written for engineers. Every surface uses internal product jargon ("Flow," "Variables," "Publish v13," "Qualifier," "Objection Handler"). A small business owner reads none of this as English.
- **Forms & Input:** Unlabeled textareas. Unguarded publish button. Simulator that might be affecting production. Critical gaps that could break her business if she clicked wrong.
- **Visual & Layout:** One page (home) is 95% empty; one page (flow builder) is 300% overstuffed. Nothing in between. No visual hierarchy telling her "look here first."
- **Feedback & State:** No bot status, no activity counts, no success signals, no loading states, and the one page she'd use for feedback (Conversations) crashed.
- **Trust & Safety:** No pause button, no confirmation on publish, no pricing/branding/support links, no data handling info, no login context. Would not trust with real customer conversations.
- **Accessibility:** Icon-only buttons with decorative glyphs; unlabeled duplicate close buttons; assumes a large monitor and fresh eyes.

## The Priya Question, Answered

> _Can someone who's already overwhelmed use this without adding to her mental load?_

**No.** Every page adds load. The dashboard gives her zero information. The conversations page crashes. The flow builder is an engineering surface. There is no path where she can open the app, learn the bot is healthy, see any number that matters, and close the tab — all in under 5 minutes.

> _Are the most important things surfaced?_

**No.** The three things Priya needs — "Is the bot on?", "What did it do in the last 24 hours?", "Does anything need me?" — are nowhere on any page.

> _Can she do what she needs in under 5 minutes?_

**No.** She can't even get into her conversations (crash). She can't pause the bot (no button). She can't tell if it's working (no status).

> _Does the app respect that she has a business to run?_

**No.** It is built as if she has hours to explore a canvas tool. She has ten minutes between customers and a phone call with her accountant after that.

---

## What Would Change the Score to a 4

1. Dashboard shows, above the fold: bot status (ON/OFF/Paused), last 24h reply count, last 7d booking count, 1-click pause. No flow builder required.
2. Conversations page actually loads, shows a list of recent threads with "bot replied" / "needs human" badges, and a 1-click "take over this thread" button.
3. Flow builder is hidden behind a "Configure bot (advanced)" link, not the primary surface.
4. Persistent left nav with plain-English labels: Home · Conversations · Bot Settings · Billing · Help.
5. Kill-switch (pause bot) accessible from every page in the top-right.
6. Clear "you are editing, not publishing" signaling and a confirm modal on publish.
7. Plain-English empty and error states ("We couldn't load your conversations — the bot is still running. Try again in a moment or contact support at support@…").

Until then, Priya closes the tab and goes back to answering her own DMs.
