# David — Accessibility-Dependent User (45)

> Low vision (200% browser zoom + NVDA screen reader). Motor impairment — keyboard-only, no mouse. Knows tech well; depends on the implementation getting it right.

I tested the Flow Builder with my standard setup: Firefox at 200% page zoom, NVDA running, no pointer at all.

**Bottom line: I cannot use this product.** Not "with effort." The team built a graph editor where the spatial graph is the primary interface, the simulator is a floating overlay with no focus management, and at my zoom level the entire surface is gated behind a "needs desktop" wall. Two of those three are fixable. The third — the mobile gate — locks me out before I get to find out.

To be fair: there is real accessibility thought here. `IconButton` enforces a `label` prop. Canvas nodes have `aria-label`, arrow-key nudging, Enter/Space to open the inspector, Escape to blur. The PageNav and Inspector tabs implement a roving-tabindex pattern with Home/End. There is a "Skip to main content" link. Someone here knows what they're doing.

The gap is that the parts that _are_ accessible are surrounded by parts that _aren't_, and the unfixed parts are blockers, not polish.

## What would change my mind

If only three things change, the score moves to 3:

1. **Fix the zoom gate.** Detect zoom-vs-screen or don't gate above 1024 _device_ px. As fallback, ship a keyboard-and-SR-friendly read-only flow viewer.
2. **Make every overlay a real dialog.** Inspector, Simulator, PromptReader: add `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, move focus on open, restore on close, trap Tab, handle Escape. Same fix three times.
3. **Announce state changes.** Toast → `role="status" aria-live="polite"`. Loading inbox cards → `aria-busy`. Trigger warning → `role="alert"`. Canvas summary → `role="status"`.

If those land, I can do my job slowly. To get to 4, fix focus visibility (#3, #9) and make canvas edges keyboard-reachable (#2).

I'm not asking for the canvas to stop being a canvas. I'm asking for the data underneath — the graph, routes, conditions — to be reachable through structured controls when the canvas itself isn't. That data already exists; it's the rendering channel that fails me.
