# Persona Review: Alex — Developer Who Judges Everything

## Summary

- **Pages reviewed:** 9
- **Issues:** 41
- **Blockers:** 5
- **Overall gut feel:** **2 / 5** — there is real craft here (roving tabindex on tabs, ARIA labels on tabpanels, focus management in `Field`), but the moment I open DevTools I see a render loop screaming 30+ times during a single click-through, the simulator's primary CTA is dead in its default state, the palette drawer eats clicks on the canvas underneath it, and "tabs" are stateful pills that don't change the URL. Four hard-engineering smells in five minutes.

## What I'd fix first

1. **Kill the render loop** (#1). Wrap `onDone` in `useCallback` in `index.tsx:131-133` and audit `flow-draft-sync.tsx` deps.
2. **Move tabs into the URL** (#3). Either `?tab=bot` or path segment.
3. **Reflow palette** (#6). Change from `position: absolute` to flex sibling. Canvas resizes, click-eating goes away.
4. **Fix simulator's primary CTA** (#2 + #15). Auto-focus textarea, hide Send until input, or have starter pills _fill_ the input.
5. **Strip engineering jargon** (#25, #33). "Saved to Supabase," `src/lib/prompts/sections/*.ts`, "until flow_id lands on the conversations table" — none belong in marketer UI.
