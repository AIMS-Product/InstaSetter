# Persona 07 — Jake (Mobile-First, Thumb-Only)

**Who I am:** 22, college, phone is my primary computer. I text while I walk, I'm in and out of apps 100x a day. Viewport is 375×667 and it had better behave like the app was built for it, not squeezed into it. If I have to pinch-zoom, I'm done.

**What I'm reviewing:** How InstaSetter survives on a real phone screen. Touch targets, reflow, horizontal scroll, input types, state preservation, readability.

---

## TL;DR

This product is NOT built for mobile. Home and 404 pages scale fine because they're practically empty. Dashboard is a placeholder. But the moment I hit the actual product — Flow Builder — it's a desktop-only UI squeezed into a phone and it falls apart spectacularly. Overlapping panels, content bleeding off-screen on both sides, tabs clipped, a modal that covers the page it was supposedly augmenting, text that requires horizontal scroll to read. I cannot use this app on my phone. Period.

Conversations is broken entirely — 500 error page. Can't review what doesn't render.

If this is meant to be a marketer-facing dashboard, and marketers live on their phones (they do — checking IG DMs IS a phone activity), this is a ship-stopper.

## Top 5 Fixes Jake Would Ship First

1. **Flow Builder needs a real mobile breakpoint.** At ≤768px: collapse multi-panel into tabs (Design / Canvas / Simulator as separate full-screen views with a bottom tab bar or top segmented control). No more overlapping panels.
2. **Kill horizontal scroll everywhere.** Nothing should bleed past 375px. Canvas should either zoom-to-fit or be replaced by a vertical list of nodes on mobile.
3. **Add a persistent mobile nav.** Bottom tab bar with Home / Conversations / Flows. Every social-media-adjacent product has this. Going back to Home between every section is punishing.
4. **Fix the conversations page.** Unblock the 500 error (looks like a missing `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` env var). Then: proper mobile list view with swipe-to-archive or at least a tappable card list.
5. **Replace Unicode icon buttons with real icons + labels.** Every icon-only button needs an accessible label, a 44×44 target, and ideally a visible short label on mobile where hover tooltips don't exist.
