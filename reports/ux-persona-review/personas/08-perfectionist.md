# Persona 08 — Claire, The Perfectionist

**Age:** 37
**Role:** Senior UX Designer, Fortune 500 (Enterprise SaaS)
**Lens:** Design system integrity. Tokenisation. Consistency of spacing, radius, weight, and colour across every surface.
**Temperament:** Quiet. Pedantic. Unforgiving of "shipped the PR without aligning with the system." Cannot unsee misalignment.

---

## Opening note (me, as Claire)

I walked through five surfaces — `/`, `/dashboard`, `/dashboard/conversations`, `/dashboard/flows/ig-organic-dm`, and `/this-route-does-not-exist` — and I counted **at least three distinct visual languages** bolted together inside one product. That is not a style choice. That is the absence of a design system.

- **Style A — Tailwind marketing.** Home, Dashboard index, 404, and the conversations error-boundary. Cream background (`#EFEBE3`-ish), Inter/system sans, `rounded-md` / `rounded-lg` buttons, ink-black primary button, subdued copy. This is the only style that appears restrained and intentional, and even here the _same string_ ("InstaSetter") is rendered at two completely different H1 sizes on `/` vs `/dashboard`.
- **Style B — error fallback.** The conversations page at `/dashboard/conversations` is, right now, **not a conversations page**. It is an error-boundary rendering "Something went wrong / An unexpected error occurred. / Try again". The fact that this is what ships when a user clicks the primary CTA on the home page is the single most damning finding in this review. There is no list, no skeleton, no empty state — only a generic error card. And even the error card quietly uses a _different_ border-radius on its button (closer to `12px`) than the home button (`rounded-md` ≈ `6px`). A perfectionist notices instantly.
- **Style C — flow-builder product surface.** A completely separate design universe. Inline-styled panels with hard-coded hex colours, `border-radius: 12px / 14px`, a purple (`#6D5DFB`-ish) and cyan accent palette that appears nowhere else in the product, an Instrument Serif feel on some strings vs Inter elsewhere, and — most egregious — a navigation bar that mixes **geometric Unicode glyphs** (`⎔ ◉ ∥ ⟳ ◐ ⤢ ⊞ ⟶ ↗`) as icons instead of a real icon set. These are not icons. They are typographic characters from five different Unicode blocks, each rendered at whatever weight and baseline the system font decides. On my 1280-wide viewport the `∥` sits noticeably higher than `◉`. It reads as a prototype, not a product.

The gap between Style A and Style C is so large that the conversations and flow-builder pages appear to belong to a different company than the home page. That is the headline.

## Gut-feel scores (1–5)

- `/` (Home) — **3.** Clean, restrained, legible. But the H1 doesn't match the dashboard's H1 and the primary CTA dumps the user onto a broken page. Polish without testing the click-path.
- `/dashboard` — **2.** Minimal to the point of being unfinished. The primary control isn't even clearly a button. The H1 reuses the product name instead of naming the section.
- `/dashboard/conversations` — **1.** Not a page. A generic error boundary rendering as the destination of the home page's primary CTA. This is the lowest score I give a live surface.
- `/dashboard/flows/ig-organic-dm` — **2.** Dense, ambitious, and visibly a different product than the marketing site. Unicode-icon navigation, custom hex colours, pointer-event bugs on every tab, no visible save state, and a Publish button with no confirmation. The ambition is clear; the execution is prototype-grade.
- `/404` — **4.** The most polished page in the product. Consistent with `/` typography (same type, same button shape, same palette). Ironically the best-tested surface because error boundaries always are.

**Overall: 2.** The marketing-layer surfaces are restrained and _could_ become the foundation of a design system. The product surfaces (conversations, flow-builder) currently belong to a different codebase and a different aesthetic. Until a single design system governs both halves of the app — shared tokens for radius, spacing, type, colour, icons, and buttons — the product will feel like two unfinished products stapled together.

---

## What I would do on day one

1. **Define tokens.** One radius scale, one spacing scale, one type scale, one palette (including semantic `status.*` colours), one motion scale. Publish them in `@/lib/design-tokens` and kill every inline `style={{ borderRadius: '12px' }}` in the flow-builder.
2. **Replace Unicode icons** with a real icon set (Lucide is already idiomatic for this stack). Every icon-only button gets an `aria-label`.
3. **Audit every `<div onClick>` in the flow-builder** and convert to `<button>` with keyboard, focus ring, and role. This fixes the pointer-intercept mess _and_ the accessibility failure in one pass.
4. **Design three states for every list surface** (loading skeleton / empty / error) before shipping the happy path. Starting with `/dashboard/conversations`, which is currently shipping state three only and calling it done.
5. **Put a confirmation + diff modal behind "Publish v13"** before anyone else touches it.
6. **Fix the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` Zod error** so it stops appearing in the browser console. That error reaching the client is a separate, trust-eroding problem.
7. **Decide whether the flow-builder is desktop-only and say so.** If not, design the responsive collapse. Silent breakage is not a strategy.
