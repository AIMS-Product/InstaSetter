# Claire — Detail-Obsessed Perfectionist (37)

UX designer, F500. I open a product and I cannot unsee the seams. Forgive me — but every misalignment, every typeface drift, every "almost-but-not-quite" pill is a paper cut, and this app has many. The work here is _clearly_ thoughtful. Someone cared. But care without a system is decoration, not design. Below is what I cannot stop noticing.

## Summary observations

If I had to give the team one note: **build the pill primitive first, ban inline styles second, and write down the eyebrow rule third.** Three components, properly tokenised, would resolve probably 18 of the 34 findings above.

The one that would give me the most confidence the team is serious is **Finding #27** (mobile gate inline styles). Refactoring that single file from inline `style={}` to design tokens forces the question "do those tokens exist?" — and the answer right now is "not really." That's the foundation problem; everything else is symptom.

The work I see here is more polished than 90% of what crosses my desk. It just isn't _done_. Inconsistency is what separates "almost-done" from "ready." This needs another pass.
