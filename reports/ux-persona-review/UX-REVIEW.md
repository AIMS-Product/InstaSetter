> **Status legend:** `✅ DONE 2026-04-28` — fixed and verified (type-check + 395 tests + lint pass). Original finding text retained for context; do not delete.
> **Status legend:** `◐ PARTIAL 2026-04-28` — a tactical slice shipped and was verified, but the original finding still has unresolved scope.

The flow builder is a polished prototype that has shipped its dev-diary to its users. Every persona — from a 78-year-old grandmother to a competitor's power user, from an exec with 30 seconds to a developer with DevTools open — independently arrived at the same conclusion: this looks like an internal staging tool, not a product. Three forces compound:

1. **The chrome leaked engineering** — "Saved to Supabase," "setter-v2," `src/lib/prompts/sections/*.ts`, "until flow_id lands on the conversations table," "DEV" badge, and "not wired yet" banners were the original trust breakers; the customer-facing leaks are now cleaned or hidden.
2. **The most important controls are incomplete** — a visible runtime bot status pill, Help menu, Inbox default, `/dashboard/flows` index, valid-flow 404 guard, Inbox search/date/status/scope filters, in-app pause/resume, persistent delete undo, mobile monitor controls, plain-language copy, and floating-panel accessibility have shipped. Remaining gaps: rollback and per-thread human takeover.
3. **The runtime is on fire** — 30+ "Maximum update depth exceeded" React errors per session, the simulator's primary `Send` button is permanently disabled, and the palette drawer eats clicks on the canvas underneath it.

A bright spot: the _idea_ is genuinely differentiated. The inline LLM Simulator (Mike's review compares favourably to ManyChat, Voiceflow, Botpress), the Variables hierarchy with explicit cardinality (Brand/Contact/Conversation), and the Bot tab's fixed-vs-editable persona governance model are all stronger than what most competitors ship. The mobile gate copy is the single warmest piece of writing in the entire app — every persona praised it. The product underneath is interesting; the packaging is still thin for anyone who is not already a customer.
